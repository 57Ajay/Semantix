# Weeknight chicken curry

Serves 4, about 40 minutes.

- 700 g chicken thighs, in pieces
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic and a thumb of ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp curry paste (or 1 tbsp garam masala, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, chilli)
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tin coconut milk
- Spinach and coriander to finish

Fry the onions slowly until golden, 10 minutes. Add garlic, ginger and the paste, cook
one minute. Add the chicken and brown it. Tomatoes and coconut milk in, simmer 20 minutes
until the sauce thickens. Stir in the spinach at the end. Serve with rice or naan.

Freezes well for up to three months.
