# Cold brew coffee

- 100 g coarsely ground coffee (like for a French press)
- 1 litre cold filtered water

Stir together in a jar, cover, leave in the fridge for 16 to 18 hours. Strain through a
paper filter. This is a concentrate: mix one part with one part water or milk over ice.
Keeps for a week in the fridge.

Medium roast works better than dark roast, which turns bitter.
