# Banana pancakes

Good use for overripe bananas. Makes about 10.

- 2 ripe bananas, mashed
- 2 eggs
- 150 g flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 200 ml milk
- Pinch of salt and cinnamon

Whisk the eggs and milk into the mashed banana, then fold in the dry ingredients. Do not
overmix, lumps are fine. Medium heat, a little butter, about 2 minutes per side, flip when
bubbles appear on top. Serve with yoghurt and maple syrup.
