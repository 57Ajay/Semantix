# Sourdough loaf

Makes one large loaf. Start the evening before.

## Starter

Feed the starter 1:1:1 (starter, flour, water) about 6 hours before mixing. It is ready
when it has doubled and a spoonful floats in water.

## Dough

- 500 g bread flour (or 450 g bread flour and 50 g whole wheat)
- 375 g water (75 percent hydration)
- 100 g active starter
- 10 g salt

1. Mix flour and water, rest for one hour (autolyse).
2. Add starter and salt, squeeze through until combined.
3. Four sets of stretch and folds, 30 minutes apart.
4. Bulk ferment until about 50 percent bigger, 4 to 6 hours at room temperature.
5. Shape, put in a floured basket, refrigerate overnight.

## Bake

Preheat the oven with a Dutch oven inside to 250 C. Bake covered 20 minutes, then
uncovered at 230 C for 25 minutes until deep brown. Cool for an hour before cutting.
