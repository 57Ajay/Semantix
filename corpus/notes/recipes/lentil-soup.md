# Red lentil soup

Cheap, vegan, done in 30 minutes.

- 250 g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1.2 litres vegetable stock
- Juice of half a lemon

Soften the vegetables in olive oil for 8 minutes, add garlic and spices, then lentils and
stock. Simmer 20 minutes until the lentils fall apart. Blend half of it for a creamy
texture, keep the rest chunky. Lemon juice and salt at the end.
