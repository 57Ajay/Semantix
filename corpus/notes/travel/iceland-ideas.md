# Iceland ideas

Winter trip to see the northern lights, ideally February or March.

- Drive the south coast from Reykjavik: Seljalandsfoss (you can walk behind the
  waterfall) and Skogafoss, black sand beach at Reynisfjara (dangerous waves, stay back).
- Glacier lagoon at Jokulsarlon, ice cave tour only with a guide.
- Northern lights: check the aurora forecast and cloud cover each evening, drive away from
  town lights.
- Rent a 4x4 with studded tyres. Roads close in storms; check the road conditions site.
- Blue Lagoon is expensive; the Secret Lagoon is quieter.
