# Utah road trip, October 2023

## Route

Salt Lake City, then south to Moab (4 hours). Five nights in Moab, two in Bryce, back
through Capitol Reef.

## Highlights

- Arches: Delicate Arch at sunset. Timed entry ticket needed, booked a month ahead.
- Canyonlands, Island in the Sky: Mesa Arch at sunrise, very crowded at the rim.
- Rented mountain bikes in Moab for a day on the Brand trails. Slickrock is grippy but the
  sand sections were hard work.
- Bryce Canyon: Navajo Loop and Queen's Garden, cold in the morning, below freezing.

## Notes for next time

- Bring more water than seems reasonable, 4 litres per person per day of hiking.
- Book the campsite in Bryce early, it was full.
