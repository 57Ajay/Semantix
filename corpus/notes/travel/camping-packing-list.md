# Camping packing list

## Shelter and sleep

- Tent, footprint, stakes
- Sleeping bag (rated to -5 C for spring and autumn)
- Sleeping pad, inflatable pillow

## Kitchen

- Stove, two gas canisters, lighter and matches
- Pot, mugs, sporks, biodegradable soap
- Water filter and two 1-litre bottles
- Bear canister where required

## Other

- Headlamp and spare batteries
- First aid kit, sunscreen, insect repellent
- Rain jacket, warm hat, extra socks
- Map, the offline map downloaded on the phone, power bank
