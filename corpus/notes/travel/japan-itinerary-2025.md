# Japan, April 2025

Flights: San Francisco to Tokyo Narita, 14 April, back 29 April.

## Plan

- Tokyo (5 nights): Asakusa, Shibuya, day trip to Nikko. teamLab exhibition, book tickets.
- Hakone (1 night): ryokan with hot spring, view of Mount Fuji if the weather is clear.
- Kyoto (5 nights): Fushimi Inari early in the morning, Arashiyama bamboo grove, Nishiki
  market. Day trip to Nara for the deer.
- Osaka (3 nights): street food in Dotonbori, fly home from Kansai.

## Practical

- Rail pass is no longer worth it for this route; buy single Shinkansen tickets.
- Get a transit card on the phone for trains and convenience stores.
- Cherry blossoms may be over in Tokyo by mid April but still out in the mountains.
