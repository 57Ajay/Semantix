# Photography tips

- Exposure triangle: aperture (depth of field), shutter speed (motion blur), ISO (noise).
- Landscapes: f/8 to f/11, low ISO, tripod at dawn or dusk. Focus a third into the scene.
- Moving animals or sport: shutter at least 1/1000 s, continuous autofocus.
- Golden hour: the hour after sunrise and before sunset, soft warm light, long shadows.
- Night sky: widest aperture, ISO 3200, about 15 seconds, manual focus on a bright star.
- Fireworks: tripod, f/8, ISO 100, bulb mode, open the shutter for 2 to 4 seconds per burst.
- Shoot RAW when the light is difficult; it keeps more detail in highlights.
