# Notes: The Art of War (Sun Tzu)

Read the Giles translation. Short, thirteen chapters.

- Know the enemy and know yourself, and you need not fear the result of a hundred battles.
- The best victory is the one won without fighting; attacking cities is the worst option.
- Long campaigns exhaust the state. Win quickly.
- Use the terrain: chapters on the nine kinds of ground and on marching are the most
  practical.
- Spies: the chapter on intelligence argues that money spent on spies is the cheapest
  investment in war.

Surprisingly applicable to negotiations and competition at work, though people quote it
too much.
