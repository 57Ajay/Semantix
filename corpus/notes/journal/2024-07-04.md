# 4 July 2024

Drove up to the lake with Sam and the neighbours' kids. Swam in the afternoon, the water
was colder than expected. Grilled corn and burgers at the picnic area.

After dark the town set off fireworks over the water; we watched from the dock. The kids
counted the big ones and lost track somewhere past forty. Tried photos with the phone,
mostly blurry streaks, one decent shot of a red burst reflected in the lake.

Home after midnight, traffic was terrible.
