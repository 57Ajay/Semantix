# 19 October 2024

Long hike on the ridge trail, about 16 km. The maples were at their peak, the whole
valley orange and red. Cold wind at the top, needed the gloves.

On the way down, just before dusk, an owl sat on a low branch next to the path and did
not move while we walked past. Big ear tufts, yellow eyes. Probably a great horned owl.

Knees sore. Bath and early night.
