# Insurance claim: stolen bicycle

- Stolen from the bike rack outside the train station on 3 June 2024, between 8:00 and
  18:30. The lock was cut.
- Police report filed online the same evening, reference in the claim email.
- Claim submitted 5 June with the purchase receipt (gravel bike, bought 2022) and photos.
- Insurer asked for the frame number, found it on the original receipt.
- Payout 1,150 after the deductible, received 2 July.

Next bike: register the frame number, use two different locks, park inside the station.
