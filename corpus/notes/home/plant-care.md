# Plant care

- Monstera (living room): water once a week when the top 3 cm of soil are dry. Wipe the
  leaves monthly. Moved it away from the radiator in winter.
- Fiddle leaf fig: hates being moved. Water every 10 days, bright indirect light. Dropped
  three leaves after the move, recovered by October.
- Succulents on the kitchen windowsill: every two weeks in summer, once a month in winter.
- Basil and mint on the balcony: water every morning in July and August.

Fertilize everything once a month from April to September, half strength.
Repot the monstera next spring, it has roots coming out of the bottom.
