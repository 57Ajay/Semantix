# Moving checklist (new apartment, 1 August)

## Four weeks before

- Give notice to the landlord in writing (done 28 June)
- Book movers: three quotes, chose the one with insurance included
- Start packing books and winter clothes

## Two weeks before

- Transfer electricity and internet to the new address, keep the old ones until 2 August
- Change address with the bank, employer, insurance and the post office forwarding service
- Use up the freezer

## Moving day

- Photos of every room in the old apartment for the deposit
- Meter readings at both places
- Keys handed over, get a receipt
